
-- the DEBUG.TXT switch ----------------------------------------------
The startup path at 0x801502F4 asks the CD for \DEBUG.TXT;1 before it
does anything else, and stores 8 into the word at 0x80174AD0 if the file
is there and 4 if it is not.  Nothing else on the disc mentions the name.
  \DEBUG.TXT;1 present on this disc: no

-- seventeen bytes of the decoder dictionary are never initialised ---
The method-3 decoder at 0x80150D4C builds its 4096-byte ring on the
stack, clears indices 0..4078, fills 0..3839 with a synthetic table,
and starts writing at 4079.  Indices 4079..4095 are left holding
whatever the previous call left on the stack.  A block that referenced
them would decode differently on every call; over 6,638 blocks on this
disc, none does.

-- filler ------------------------------------------------------------
all-zero sectors in the data track: 154 of 220544 (0.07%)
DUMMY3M.DA is declared at LBA 220694 for 36,952,064 bytes (18043 sectors).
The data track ends at LBA 220543, so the whole extent lies outside it:
LBA 220694 is exactly where track 2, the CD-DA audio track, starts after
its 150-sector pre-gap, and 220694 + 18043 = 238737 is the volume size the PVD
declares.  The pad file names the music track.

printable runs that read as text, out of 14 distinct runs in all:
   14487 x  'rogram Manager'
     440 x  'rTE4-'
      10 x  'Execute'

"rogram Manager" is the tail of "Program Manager", the title of the
Windows shell window.  The machine that authored this XA stream was
running Windows, and the tool wrote its uncleared buffer into every
unused slot of the interleave.
